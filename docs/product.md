# Product vision

Urbuddy is a production-grade, single-user-first, local-first personal
multilingual language intelligence application. Its initial audience is
non-native English-speaking professionals.

Its mission is to help users perceive, understand, communicate,
remember, and learn from real conversations. The primary use case is a
professional participating in English-speaking remote meetings who
needs real-time comprehension, contextual translation, communication
assistance, and adaptive language learning.

The long-term objective is increased user independence: the user
becomes able to understand and speak English without assistance,
instead of depending on the tool permanently.

Personalized defaults belong in configuration. Source and target languages
are user-facing choices, and capability claims depend on qualification for
the relevant language and workflow.

## Target user

A non-native English-speaking professional (for example a backend
engineer) who joins fast, accented, technically dense English meetings
and needs to follow them live, respond naturally, and convert daily
work conversations into personal language progress.

## Primary workflows

1. Join a meeting, follow the live bilingual caption stream, and stay
   oriented when speech is fast, accented, or overlapping.
2. Recover missed context on demand: what was decided, what a term
   meant, what needs a response.
3. Draft or refine an English response before speaking or writing it.
4. Review personal vocabulary and comprehension gaps after the meeting,
   and practice them through spaced repetition.

## Goals

- Real-time comprehension of live technical conversation.
- Stable, trustworthy translation that preserves negation, numbers,
  commitments, and technical terms.
- Communication assistance grounded in the actual meeting context.
- Durable personal memory of vocabulary and difficulty, owned by the
  user.
- Measurable progress toward unaided comprehension.

## Non-goals

- A generic chatbot or meeting recorder.
- A SaaS platform, multi-tenant service, or subscription product.
- An autonomous meeting participant that speaks on the user's behalf.
- A transcription leaderboard entry: benchmark scores are proxies,
  comprehension is the metric.

## Success criteria

The system succeeds when the user understands more of each meeting
unaided over time, translation errors that change meaning trend toward
zero on the mandatory regression corpus, no acknowledged transcript is
ever lost, and no data leaves the device without explicit
authorization.
